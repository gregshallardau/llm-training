Never share with AI — danger categories
🩹
Personal Health Info
Medical records, diagnoses, prescriptions, patient data — protected by law in most jurisdictions.
🔐
Passwords & Credentials
API keys, login passwords, tokens, SSH keys, database credentials of any kind.
👤
Client PII
Real names, home addresses, ID numbers, email addresses linked to identifiable individuals.
💵
Financial Account Details
Bank account numbers, credit card numbers, BSBs, trading account data.
📄
Confidential Contracts
NDAs, client agreements, unreleased deal terms, or proprietary legal documents.
🛡️
Internal Security Info
Network diagrams, vulnerability reports, firewall rules, incident response plans.
Safe vs unsafe — sharing spectrum
Safe to Share
  • General writing and editing tasks
  • Publicly available information
  • Fully anonymized examples
  • Generic code and technical questions
  • Brainstorming with no real data
Use Caution
  • Internal processes (no proprietary detail)
  • Anonymized client scenarios
  • Business logic with names removed
  • Drafts referencing internal policy
  • Check your org's AI policy first
Do Not Share
  • Real client data or records
  • Credentials and secrets
  • Sensitive financial details
  • Confidential business info
  • Health or legal records
Privacy best practices
1
Use enterprise or private deployment
Org-managed AI tools have data retention controls. Prefer these over personal consumer accounts.
2
Anonymize before pasting
Replace real names, IDs, and addresses with placeholders like [CLIENT_NAME] before submitting.
3
Check your org's AI policy
Many organisations have specific rules about what data may be sent to external AI services.
4
Opt out of training if available
Most providers allow you to disable use of your data for training — enable this in account settings.
5
Treat prompts like emails
Assume your prompt could be seen by others. If you'd hesitate to send it in email, don't type it in a prompt.

💡 Key Insight

The rule of thumb: if you wouldn't paste it into an email to a stranger, don't paste it into an AI prompt. Use anonymized examples. Your org's data is your responsibility.
