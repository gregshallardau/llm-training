Module 15

Copilot in Context

How is Copilot different from ChatGPT?

1   Standalone Chat vs In-App AI

Standalone Chat

ChatGPT  ·  Claude.ai  ·  Gemini
  • ⚠ You bring the context — copy & paste required
  • ↕ Works outside your apps, in a browser tab
  • ✎ Great for open-ended drafting from scratch
  • ⚠ Data leaves your org unless enterprise plan
  • ↺ Manual loop: write → copy → paste → revise
VS

In-App Copilot

Word  ·  Excel  ·  Teams  ·  Outlook
  • ✔ Context already there — Copilot sees the doc/email
  • ✔ Lives inside the app you're already working in
  • ✔ Aware of your meeting, spreadsheet, or inbox
  • ✔ Enterprise data protection built-in (Microsoft 365)
  • ✔ Prompt in place — no copy-paste needed

4   When to Use What
→ Standalone
"I need to generate something from scratch"
Use ChatGPT, Claude.ai, or Copilot Chat
No existing document to reference. You're exploring ideas, brainstorming structures, or writing from a blank canvas.
→ In-App Copilot
"I'm already working in a doc, email, or meeting"
Use Copilot in Word / Outlook / Teams
The context is right there. Let Copilot read your document and act on it — no copy-paste, no context switching.
→ In-App Copilot
"My data is sensitive or work-confidential"
Use M365 Copilot (enterprise plan)
Your data stays within Microsoft's enterprise boundary. It is not used to train models and is covered by your organisation's compliance policies.

💡 Key Insight

The power of in-app Copilot is that the context is already there. You don't need to copy-paste your document — Copilot can see it. This changes the workflow from "extract and prompt" to "prompt in place", cutting out the friction steps that slow down standalone chat tools and keeping you focused inside the app where the work actually happens.
