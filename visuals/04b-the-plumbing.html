The Fundamental Problem

An LLM is trained once, on a snapshot of the world. After training, it knows nothing new. It cannot look at your SharePoint, read your internal policies, or know what happened in a meeting yesterday.

Option A: Fine-Tuning

  • Train model on your data
  • Expensive and slow
  • Data gets stale quickly
  • Model memorises everything

Option B: RAG

  • Retrieve relevant documents
  • Cheap and fast
  • Always current
  • Model uses retrieved context
  • Easy to update

🎯 The Filing Cabinet Analogy

Imagine you've hired a brilliant new analyst. They're smart, great at writing, excellent at summarising — but they've never worked at your company before. You can either spend months training them (fine-tuning), or before every meeting, hand them the relevant files from the filing cabinet (RAG). RAG is Option B — it's always current, cheap, and fast.
