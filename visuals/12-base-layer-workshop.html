Module 12: The Base Layer Workshop

How do I build my own?

Workshop Flow — 15 Minutes
0 – 3 min
Context
Review the template. Think about your role, team, and what you actually do day-to-day.
3 – 8 min
Draft
Fill in each section honestly. Aim for 200–400 words total. Bullet points are fine.
8 – 12 min
Test
Paste your base layer into a new chat. Ask the AI a typical work question. Notice the difference.
12 – 15 min
Refine
Adjust anything that felt off. Remove filler. Keep what made the response better.
Template Structure — Filled Example
## WHO I AM
Name / Role: Alex Chen, Senior Operations Manager
Organisation / Team: Midlands NHS Trust — Patient Flow & Scheduling (team of 12)
## WHAT I WORK ON
Current Projects: Elective backlog recovery, winter capacity planning, bed management dashboard rollout
Regular Tasks: Weekly performance reports, stakeholder briefings, escalation notes, process documentation
## HOW I COMMUNICATE
Communication Style: Concise, plain English. Avoid jargon. Bullet points preferred for internal docs. Formal tone for exec reports.
Key Stakeholders: Chief Operating Officer, ward sisters, GP liaison leads, IT project team
## OUTPUT PREFERENCES
Preferred Format: Short paragraphs or numbered lists. Tables for comparisons. No unnecessary preamble.
Key Terminology: RTT (referral to treatment), DTOC (delayed transfer of care), four-hour standard, pathway

What Makes a Good Base Layer — Checklist
Job Title & Level Tells the AI your seniority and what kinds of decisions you make. Shapes the register and complexity of responses.
Team Size & Structure Helps the AI understand whether you are managing others, working solo, or navigating a large hierarchy.
Industry & Sector Grounds the AI in the right regulatory, cultural, and operational context for your field.
Common Tasks List the 3–5 things you ask AI to help with most often. The AI can anticipate your needs and defaults.
Preferred Output Format Bullets, tables, short paragraphs, numbered steps? Stating this once means you do not need to repeat it every prompt.
Tone Preference Formal or casual? Blunt or diplomatic? This prevents the AI defaulting to a generic corporate voice.
Key Terminology Acronyms and domain-specific terms your colleagues use. Stops the AI explaining things you already know.
Key Stakeholders Names or roles of people you regularly write for or about. Saves context-setting in every individual prompt.

Key Insight

Your base layer is like a staff profile for the AI. Once written, paste it at the start of every conversation. 200–400 words is ideal — enough context without using too much of the window.
