How do I make AI know about my data?

🧊 The Frozen Brain Problem

An LLM's knowledge is frozen at the moment training ends. Anything that happened after that — your company's latest policies, a meeting yesterday, a new product launch — is completely unknown to it. No matter how smart it is, it can't access today's information on its own.

Fine-Tuning

  • Train the model on your data
  • Expensive and slow (days/weeks)
  • Data gets stale over time
  • Model memorises everything
  • Hard to update when things change

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

  • Retrieve docs at query time
  • Cheap and fast to set up
  • Always uses current data
  • Model uses retrieved context
  • Update documents, instantly current

🎯 Key Insight

RAG is like giving the AI a reference library before it answers. Instead of relying only on what it memorised during training, it looks up relevant documents first. The better your documents are organised, the better the answers will be. Garbage in, garbage out — RAG can't fix bad source material.
