Think of It as a Brain

The unified metaphor for the entire series

📚

It has read everything

Trillions of words — books, websites, emails, code, forums. It has absorbed the patterns of how language works.

🔗

It knows patterns

Not facts, not databases — statistical relationships between words. It knows what usually comes next.

⏭️

It predicts what comes next

One word at a time, it asks "what's the most likely next word?" and keeps going until the response is complete.

⚠️ But it has amnesia

Every conversation is its first day. It doesn't know your name, your role, your team, or your clients. You're talking to a brilliant stranger every time.

✅ The solution: Write a base layer

One document about yourself. Paste it once per conversation. The AI now "knows" you — not because it remembers, but because you told it.
