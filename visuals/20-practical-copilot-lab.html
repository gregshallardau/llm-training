The kickoff — say something like this
"You're the conductor. We're going to build the quote comparison. Walk me through it one step at a time. For each step, tell me the single action to take, wait for me to do it and paste back what I get, then move on. If you need something only I can fetch, ask me for it. One beat, then wait."

The Six Builds — escalating hands-on exercises

Click any build to see the reveal — what it teaches. Suggested run order: 6 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5
1
Generate a file, not text
Outlook → .ics calendar file from a "let's meet Tuesday" email
Reveal: Copilot outputs downloadable artefacts, not just text you paste. One file drops a whole schedule of renewals into Outlook, Apple, or Google Calendar.
2
Transform, don't retype
Word — six rushed bullets → a client letter, then same content in three registers
Reveal: You steer with input structure, then iterate. Formal / plain / warm are not three documents — they're one source, three prompts. The source is gold; the output is disposable.
3
Interactive artefact from plain words
A fillable form, a Copilot Page, or a self-contained HTML tool from a description
Reveal: Copilot builds tools, not just documents. A premium calculator or onboarding checklist that actually runs when you double-click it — written in seconds, not hours in a dev tool.
4
Extract structure from chaos ⭐ centrepiece
Excel — design schema → extract from 3–5 quote PDFs → comparison charts
Reveal: Copilot reads your unstructured documents and turns them into structured, analysable data. But always verify the numbers — this is the hallucination-prone step (ties back to Module 06). Needs M365 licence + files in OneDrive/SharePoint.
5
Chain across apps
Outlook → Excel → Word — action items from thread, into tracker, into status summary
Reveal: Copilot is connective tissue. Think in pipelines. Output from one app becomes input to the next; you're assembling a workflow, not using three separate tools.
6
Triage a thread — the warm-up
Outlook — summarise a 20-email thread, list who owes what, draft a reply
Reveal: The lowest-risk first win. Reading and compressing — mistakes are cheap because you check before sending. This is where the room relaxes and believes it.
Suggested run order (~60 min): 6 (warm-up, low stakes) → 1 (first artefact) → 2 (transform) → 3 (build a tool) → 4 (the centrepiece — give it the most time) → 5 if time allows
