How BPE (Byte-Pair Encoding) Works

Start:
[t] [h] [e] [r] [e]
Step 1:
'th' is very common → merge to [th]
→
Step 2:
'the' is extremely frequent → merge to [the]
→
Result:
[the] [re] — common words become single tokens

🎯 The Rule of Thumb

1 token ≈ ¾ of a word. Or: 100 words ≈ 130 tokens. A page of text ≈ 500 tokens. Uncommon words get chopped more — jargon and unusual terms cost more tokens.
