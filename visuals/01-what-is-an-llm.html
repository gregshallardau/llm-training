🎯 The Core Insight

An LLM is a program that predicts the most likely next word, over and over, until it's written a full response. It's not searching a database — it's generating text one word at a time based on patterns.

❌ "It's searching the internet"

It has no internet access during generation. Its knowledge comes from training data with a cutoff date.

❌ "It remembers our conversation"

It re-reads the entire conversation from scratch every time. No persistent memory between messages.

❌ "It understands what I mean"

It processes statistical patterns. Whether this constitutes "understanding" is an open philosophical question.
